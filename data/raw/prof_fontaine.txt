Prof. Dr. Pirmin Fontaine
Professor for Logistics and Operations Analytics
In our research group, the main focus lies in developing efficient solution methods for problem settings in mobility, supply chain management, production, and logistics. The starting point is always real-life applications, formulated as mathematical optimization problems- Then, tailored solution methods are chosen and newly developed. Here, the focus lies on efficient reformulations, decomposition methods (e.g., Benders decomposition and branch-and-repair), and metaheuristics.
Current projects cover, for example: Increasing the resiliency in supply chains using machine learning, Improving on-demand bus systems, or planning urban logistics systems.

More information and further projects can be found here -> https://www.ku.de/en/the-ku/faculties/wfi/departments-chairs/business-administration/assistant-professorship-for-operations-management
