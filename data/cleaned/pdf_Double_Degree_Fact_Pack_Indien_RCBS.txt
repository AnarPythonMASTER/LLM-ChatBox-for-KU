Student Requirements: Admission
WFI students
• Must have completed a 3-year Bachelor’s degree (usually 180 ECTS) which included business-relatedcourses 
(e.g. on general management, statistics, economics, etc.) to ensure a basic understanding of management 
science.
• Must be full-time KU M.Sc. students
• Must have passed all semester 1 and 2 credits (60 ECTS, corresponds to 54 RCBSCP)
RCBS students
• Must have completed a 3-year Bachelor’s (usually 36 courses with a total of 120 local CP) which included 
business-related courses (e.g. on general management, statistics, economics, etc.) to ensure a basic 
understanding of management science.
• Must be full-time PGDM students at RCBS
• Must have passed all trimester 1 credits (18 RCBS CP, corresponds to 20 ECTS, including business related 
courses such as statistics, economics and principles of management etc.)

[Page 10]
Student Requirements: Study Abroad
• All participating students must adhere to the rules and regulations of their home and host
institutions.
• Participating students shall not attend courses at the host institution which they have already 
attended at their home institution.
• Participating students shall not take part in further study abroad activities (such as additional 
study abroad semesters) between KU and RCBS outside of this agreement.
• Bachelor (“undergraduate”) courses are not allowed to be taken nor is it allowed to take 
courses from trimester 1 – 3.
• For German students going to India: All compulsory courses (“Pflichtkurse”) must be passed at 
WFI.
• Students are responsible for their choice of courses.

Student Requirements: Graduation for WFI Students
Graduation requirements to obtain the degree at the host institution
• Must obtain 60 ECTS (worth 54 RCBS CP) in semesters 1 and 2 at WFI/KU, must comply with all the mandatory or
compulsory elective courses (including Studium Pro)
• Must validate two major specializations and complete all necessary academic workload of 27 RCBS CP
(corresponds to 30 ECTS) in trimester 5 and 6 at RCBS
• Must write a master thesis (30 ECTS, corresponds to 27 RCBS CP) in semester 4 at WFI with the support of a
RCBS instructor and an India-related topic and relevant academic research
•
Upon successful completion of the program…
Indian students receive – in addition to their PGDM – a Master of Science (M. Sc.) degree from WFI
WFI students receive – in addition to their M. Sc. – a PGDM from RCBS

FAQ (1/3)
1.
How proficient do I need to be inEnglish?
Currently, an English level of B2 (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) is required.
2.
Do I have to pay tuition fees? What else do I have topay?
WFI and RCBS have agreed to waive tuition for participating in this program. However, WFI will have tocharge 
membership fees (currently € 71,00) in behalf of the “Studentenwerk”, an entity which provides subsidized 
student services (such as cafeteria & restaurant). WFI is not entitled to waive these fees, because all students 
legally have to be members of “Studentenwerk”. Thus, you do not have to pay tuition fees but any other costs 
(travel, accommodation, cost of living etc.) will be at your expense.
3.
Where will I stay during my studies atRCBS?
At the RCBS guest students usually have the opportunity to stay in an international students’ house located close
to campus (see description below). RCBS’ IO will provide support in arrangingaccommodation.
4.
Who will support me locally?
Both institutions have a student buddy system, local IO and a liaison manager to support students from the 
partner university. For general questions, students should first seek support by their student buddy before 
contacting the IO or the liaisonmanager.

[Page 23]
FAQ (2/3)
5.
What should I do if I need to extend my studies (illness before / during my stay abroad or insufficient grades)? 
Both institutions accept the partner institution’s local rules and procedures (e.g. as outlined in KU’s “M.Sc. 
Prüfungsordnung”) for a possible extension of studies dueto
•
(chronic) illness or
•
insufficient grades of a student.
At WFI, students suffering from illness and requesting to extend the time of an examination or postpone it may be 
asked to obtain a medical certificate by a doctor chosen by theinstitution.
In the case of insufficient grades (at RCBS: “D” or “F”; at WFI: “5.0”), students may repeat their exam twice at 
WFI and once at RCBS before a course is finally determined as “failed”. Students who have obtained non-
passing grades are generally responsible for fulfilling any local requirements (e.g. physical presence) when 
repeating an exam. Exceptions may only be granted within the institution’s examination rules and procedures
6.
How should I prepare for my stayabroad?
Besides logistical details (visa, housing, vaccination, transportation, transcripts etc.), it is important to prepare
culturally for the study abroad. This may include additional language training or reading about local culture, 
customs, geography, and law. In addition, students should frequently interact with their Indian program mates. 
For logistical planning, please get in touch with the IO early. All other preparation is expected to be performed 
autonomously by students.

FAQ (3/3)
7.
How should I behave when studying at RCBS?
As guests and representatives of the WFI, all participating students have to strictly comply with any local rules
and regulations provided by RCBS, law or government officials. This applies to all behavior and particularly to
the use of alcohol and drugs. Smoking in public is not allowed in India. Do hide yourself whilesmoking.
8.
Are scholarships available to support theprogram?
Please note that students in this program recently had problems obtaining Auslands-BAföG. 
Students can apply individually with the DAAD (https://www2.daad.de/ausland/studieren/stipendium/de/70-
stipendien-finden-und-bewerben/), with BayIND (https://www.bayind.de/de/programme-foerderungen/ ), Ku 
IntAid (https://www.ku.de/international/studierende-der-ku/studienaufenthalt-im-ausland/finanzierung/foerderung-
durch-die-ku/ku-intaid), policy-related or other scholarship providers. 
9.
Can I continue my studies in India after having graduated with thePGDM?
Generally no. The PGDM is a practice-oriented degree similar to the MBA and does not automatically qualify for
e.g. doctoralstudies.
