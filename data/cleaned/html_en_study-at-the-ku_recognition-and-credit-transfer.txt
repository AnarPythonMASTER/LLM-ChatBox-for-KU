Online Accreditation Portal
Please submit your application for accreditation or credit transfer exclusively using our online credit transfer portal.
If you are enrolled in a degree program that does not yet use the digital online procedure—which currently applies only to the Magister program in Catholic Theology, the Digital Bachelor’s in Catholic Theology, and certain certificate programs—please contact the
Board of Examiners
responsible for your program directly.
Once your application has been approved by the Board of Examiners, you may only retake the module if multiple enrollment or grade improvement is expressly permitted for the module under the examination regulations applicable to you. Upon successful completion of the recognition or credit transfer process, the course will be recorded in
KU.Campus
, and the result will appear on your individual overview of grades.
