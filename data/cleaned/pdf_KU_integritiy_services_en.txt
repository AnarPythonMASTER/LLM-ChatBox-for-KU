How does the pme Familienservice help you?
 
Why do we now have an external ombudsman?
Companies and public authorities are obligated to establish an
anonymous whistleblower system. If you have observed
misconduct or have been affected by it yourself, you can now
report it anonymously, securely, and free of charge. Simply contact
the external experts at pme Familienservice.
You can contact the confidential office about these topics:
Discrimination
Violence
Bullying
Sexual harassment
Is your personal concern not listed?
Please feel free to call us or send an email to: 
kuei-vertrauenstelle@familienservice.de, we will find a solution!
You can report incidents. This can also be done completely anonymously!
The external experts of pme Familienservice provide advice.
They are available 24/7 during emotionally stressful events. 
If needed, the pme Family Service can help you with the incident to work
through the issues with the parties involved and to enter into dialogue
together.
