Education for Everyone
Education is a human right. It is of central importance for a functioning society and creates a wide range of opportunities - for developing one's own personality, for expanding one's own horizons, and for actively helping shape the world. At the same time, the economy, technology and society are constantly changing, and people are constantly confronted with new challenges. Education is therefore not a closed concept, but a lifelong desideratum.
For this reason, the professional development and continuing education programs offered by the Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt are open to all interested parties, regardless of ethnicity, religion, disability, immigration status or gender, even beyond a classic Bachelor's or Master's degree program. We attach great importance to building responsibility, values and sustainability, which is why lifelong learning is also a core pillar of our portfolio.
Discover our offers for interested people of all ages here and visit the KU - as a guest student, visitor to the Kinderuni (“Children's University”) or attendee of our taster days.
