Help in case of emergency

In case of danger to life and limb...

...dial the emergency number immediately! (Please make sure to predial a "0" when calling from your office phone).

Police 110
Fire brigade/rescue service 112
On-call medical service 116 117
Poison control center Bavaria 089/19240

Keep calm, get an overview of the situation and describe it to the emergency call center:

Where did something happen?
What happened?
How many people are affected?
What is the nature of the illness or injury?
Wait for questions!

On-call service of the KU

24-hour emergency number of the University +49 8421 / 9 32 32 32 or +49 800 / 9 32 32 32

You can reach the KU's on-call service 24/7 with this number. Please only use this number in emergency situations – for example in the following crisis situations or security threats. In case of imminent danger to life and limb, please always call the emergency number 110 first.

What to do in an emergency

Killing spree, hostage situation, use of weapons

Flee

  • Escape from the danger zone
  • Help other people to escape
  • Do not go to the usual meeting point
  • Look for suitable cover, e.g. thick wall
  • Warn other people and tell them to flee as well

Hide

  • Barricade in suitable rooms
  • Keep quiet, lights and sound off
  • Switch your mobile phone off or mute
  • Lie flat on the floor
  • Stay away from windows and doors
  • Look for suitable cover, e.g. thick wall

Call the police: 110

Threat / harassment / violence

If you are attacked, the following advice can help you to escape critical situations unharmed:

  • Defend yourself massively! Scream and shout to make others aware of your situation, defend yourself physically (kicks, punches, scratches etc.). In most cases, this will cause the offender to interrupt and stop.
  • Distance yourself with words. Address the offender formally (with the German “Sie”), using their last name. When addressing the offender informally by using their first name, you reduce the distance.
  • Search for a possibility to escape.
  • Specifically address people nearby to make them aware.
  • Use your bike as a barrier between you and the offender or use it as a means to escape.
  • Also if the incident happens on campus or University premises, call the police immediately (110) and file a charge in any case. 95 percent of all criminal offenses are solved through witness statements.

If you injure the offender when defending yourself, this will fall under the self-defense act (Notwehrgesetz, section 32 German Criminal Code). This also applies if you help a person who is being attacked (emergency assistance). However, the defensive action must be appropriate and reasonable in view of the situation.

If you witness someone being threatened or violently attacked:

  • Take a de-escalating approach, but do not try to stand in the way of people who are obviously prepared to use violence.
  • Draw attention to the situation by shouting and screaming. Specifically address other people nearby to make them aware. Warn other people.
  • Call the police (110).
  • If you feel threatened: lock yourself in or flee the building and try to join groups of people to feel safer in a crowd.

Fire, smoke development or smell of burning, release of hazardous substances

Keep calm and alert the fire department: Call 112
than inform the KU on-call service: 08421 / 9323232 or 0800 / 9323232

Get to safety.

  • Warn people at risk
  • Take helpless persons with you
  • Close doors and windows, but do not lock doors
  • Close fire doors
  • Follow escape route signs
  • Do not use the elevator
  • Go to meeting point
  • Follow instructions

Try to extinguish the fire

  • Use portable fire extinguishers
  • Use fire hose

Suicide: A person threatens to take their own life

Call the police: Call 110

  • Only make personal contact with the person concerned if they initiate a conversation or if they are no longer able to free themselves from a life-threatening predicament on their own

Suspicious objects, letters or parcels

Inform the on-call service of the KU: 08421 / 9323232 or 0800 / 9323232

  • Do not touch, move or open the object
Technical interruptions

Outage of communication equipment (telephone, mobile network, data communication)

  • Use alternative communication channels: Mobile phone, SMS, landline phone, KU social media channels
  • Go to KU emergency homepage: ku.statusinfo.live

Power outage

Inform the on-call service of the KU: 08421 / 9323232 or 0800 / 9323232

Ensure general safety

  • When carrying out experiments: Stop the experiment immediately. Report to the on-call service immediately if an experiment could become uncontrollable due to the power cut
  • Switch off electrical appliances and power strips
  • Keep freezers and refrigerators closed
  • Open windows and/or doors to let in fresh air
  • Use a battery-powered laptop, tablet PC or mobile phone to obtain information

Failure of water, gas or heat supply

Inform the on-call service of the KU: 08421 / 9323232 or 0800 / 9323232

  • If necessary, make the on-call service aware of possible technical hazards and working conditions that are questionable in terms of health or hygiene

Accident involving small quantities of hazardous substances

Measures to be carried out by experienced staff only:

  • Put on suitable and sufficient personal protective equipment
  • Prevent spread to other rooms: Close doors
  • Bind and absorb hazardous substance with suitable means
