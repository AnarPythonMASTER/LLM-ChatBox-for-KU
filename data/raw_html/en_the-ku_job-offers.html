IT jobs

The Computer Center is the central IT service provider of the Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt.

If your IT skills do not exactly match current job offers or if there are no job advertisements at the moment, we would still be pleased to hear from you if you are interested in working in our Computer Center. In one of these cases, please send us your application documents in one PDF file with the keyword "Initiativbewerbung" by e-mail to urz-bewerbungen(at)ku.de.
