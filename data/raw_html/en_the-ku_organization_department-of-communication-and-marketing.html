Department of Communication and Marketing

The KU’s Department of Communication and Marketing is responsible for the University’s engagement with the public and manages all of its external and internal communication. We are responsible for press and public relations work, the KU’s main website and university publications, university marketing, corporate design and event management. We advise the university management and members of the KU on topics related to public relations and are a point of contact for journalists, staff and students of the university, as well as the interested public.

Press office

News on the KU, on current research, on what happens on campus and information on events: Here, journalists will find information on different topics and experts, facts and figures as well as free pictures for their press coverage.

Information for employees

You know of a topic that would be of interest to the media? You need advice and support for updating your online presence? Or do you require layout templates, logos and photos for designing print materials? You want to announce a guest lecture or conference in the KU event calendar or need small presents for guests or greeting cards? Here, KU employees find all relevant information and downloads.

Translations

The translator at the KU translates texts from the central administration and facilities from and into English, offers a certified translation service for students and alumni and is happy to answer your linguistic questions in connection with the English corporate wording at the KU.
