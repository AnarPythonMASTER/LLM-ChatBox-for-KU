===CHUNK===
type: module
name: Applied Data Science Project
programme: data_science_master
area: required
---
The Applied Data Science Project is a required module in the Master Data Science at KU, worth 10 ECTS and normally taken in the second semester. In this module, students work in teams on a real problem from an application area. The project is typically developed and implemented together with a partner company from industry, though interdisciplinary projects with scientific partners are also possible. It consolidates the connection between data, algorithms, and modeling in a concrete application context before students choose their Master thesis topic.
