===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Campus Location of Data Science Programme
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt is based at the Ingolstadt campus, where all courses are taught. The institute is located in the renovated Georgianum building. The programme is not based in Eichstätt.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Recommended Arrival Time for New Students
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
Students in the Data Science Bachelor programme are recommended to arrive in Ingolstadt around the beginning of October. This allows time to settle in and participate in a preparatory course before regular lectures begin in mid-October.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Visa Delays and Enrollment Deadline
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
International students in the Data Science Bachelor programme may receive their visa later due to administrative processing times. KU issues admission letters as quickly as possible and sets enrollment deadlines as late as possible, usually just a few days before lectures begin. All required documents, including the visa, must be submitted to the Student Office by the enrollment deadline stated in the admission letter.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Expected Study Workload and Self-Study
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor programme requires substantial independent study in addition to lectures. For example, the Linear Algebra course includes approximately 6 contact hours and 5 hours of self-study per week. The overall workload is comparable to other German universities, and studying Data Science at KU is considered a full-time commitment.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Required Prior Mathematics Knowledge
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
Prior mathematics knowledge is helpful but not required for the Data Science Bachelor programme. Topics such as linear algebra and analysis may be challenging at first, but introductory support is provided. A preparatory course before the semester covers basic mathematical foundations. First-semester mathematics courses are designed so that all topics are introduced from the ground up.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Programme Duration and Completion Time
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt is designed to be completed within three years. The structure of the programme supports timely graduation within this standard study period.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Free German Language Courses for Students
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The university offers free German language courses to support students in achieving at least A2-level German proficiency, which must be demonstrated after one year of study. These courses are regularly offered and are often scheduled to fit into the student timetable, such as on weekday mornings.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Online or Hybrid Study Format
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor programme is primarily an on-campus degree. Some courses provide online materials or recorded lectures for independent study, but in-person participation is strongly recommended. All examinations must be taken in person at the university.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Selection and Change of Major (Specialization)
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
Students in the Data Science Bachelor programme must choose a specialization (major) by the end of the second semester. From the third semester onward, students follow courses aligned with their chosen major. It is possible to change the specialization later.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Semester Abroad Opportunities
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
Students in the Data Science Bachelor programme are encouraged to complete a semester abroad, typically in the fifth semester. The university supports students in planning and organizing study stays abroad through its international office.

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: Teaching Language and English Instruction
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor programme is taught in English, with lectures and examinations primarily conducted in English. Some examinations may be offered in German upon request. Professors can also explain content in German outside lectures. English is considered an important academic and industry skill, and additional language support courses are available.

The following lecture videos are provided as examples of English-taught content:

===CHUNK===
type: faq
name: KU Status, Funding, and Degree Recognition
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt is approximately 85% funded by the Free State of Bavaria and operates under Bavarian university regulations. Although it is considered a private university, its degrees are equivalent to those of other Bavarian universities and are internationally recognized. The Data Science Bachelor programme is internationally accredited and tuition-free.

===CHUNK===
type: structure
name: Mathematical Institute for Machine Learning and Data Science (MIDS)
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor and Master programmes at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt are coordinated by the Mathematical Institute for Machine Learning and Data Science (MIDS), founded in 2022. MIDS develops and teaches the scientific foundations for responsible use of data science and digitalization. It serves as the academic structure responsible for organizing and advancing the Data Science programmes.

New students arriving for the Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt typically travel by train to Ingolstadt after arriving in Germany.
