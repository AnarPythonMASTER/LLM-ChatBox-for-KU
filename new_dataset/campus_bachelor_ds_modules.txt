type: programme_info
name: Studium.Pro Requirement Data Science Bachelor
programme: data_science_bachelor
area: studium_pro
---
The Data Science Bachelor at KU requires one Studium.Pro module worth 5 ECTS during the whole programme. Studium.Pro is an interdisciplinary general-education area, and the chosen module does not need to be related to Data Science, mathematics, or the sciences. Students pick one module that interests them from a broad catalogue offered each semester. The one Studium.Pro option that is directly Data Science focused is "More Data, More Insights? Modern Data Science Methods from a Transdisciplinary Perspective," taught in English by a team including Dominik Stöger, David Streich, and Pirmin Fontaine; this is recommended for students who want their Studium.Pro module to connect to their main subject.

type: programme_info
name: Studium.Pro Available Topics Data Science Bachelor
programme: data_science_bachelor
area: studium_pro
---
The Studium.Pro catalogue for the Data Science Bachelor at KU offers a wide range of interdisciplinary modules each semester, most taught in German with a few in English. Typical options cover themes such as ethics, society, sustainability, and culture. Examples include: the Forum K'Universale lecture series (with recurring themes such as "Inventing Futures: From Frankenstein to Artificial Intelligence" and "War, Society and Responsibility"), Media Ethics, Man Society and Religion, Basic Questions in Medical Ethics, Gender Studies, Introduction to Ethical Education, Advanced Topics of Civic Education, Introduction to Sociology, Cultural and Educational Work, Service Learning, Sustainable Development, Sustainable Nutrition, Understanding Climate Change, Democracy and Civil Society, and The Science of Happiness (taught in English, online). Students choose one module worth 5 ECTS; the specific offerings vary between the winter and summer semesters.
