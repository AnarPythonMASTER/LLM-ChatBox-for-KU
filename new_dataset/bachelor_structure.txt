programme: data_science_bachelor
---
Applicants to the Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt are expected to have a strong background in mathematics, analytical thinking skills, and a strong interest in understanding complex problems and developing technical solutions. Required qualifications include a general university entrance qualification and English language proficiency. International students must additionally provide proof of German language proficiency at A2 level after the first year of study.

type: structure
name: Data Science Bachelor Programme Structure and ECTS System
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt is a six-semester degree requiring the completion of 180 ECTS credits. The programme is structured into a compulsory core area, a specialization (focus area), and a required elective area. It also includes a Bachelor’s seminar and a Bachelor’s thesis as integral components of the degree.

===CHUNK===
type: structure
name: Required Core Modules in Data Science Bachelor
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The compulsory core area of the Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt includes foundational modules in Data Science, mathematics, statistics, and computer science. Data Science modules include Data Lab, Ethics of Algorithms and Data, Foundations of Data Science, Foundations of Machine Learning, Hands-on Machine Learning and Data Science, and Practical Training. Mathematics includes Analysis for Data Science 1 and 2, Linear Algebra 1 and 2 with Analytic Geometry, and Optimization in Data Science. Statistics includes Introduction to Statistics, Introduction to Stochastics, and Statistical Learning. Computer science includes Introduction to Programming, Advanced Programming, Algorithms and Data Structures, and Basics of Information Systems.

The Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt allows students to choose a specialization that deepens knowledge in a selected area of data science or its application fields. The required elective area in the Data Science Bachelor programme at KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt allows students to expand their studies beyond core content or deepen interdisciplinary competencies.
