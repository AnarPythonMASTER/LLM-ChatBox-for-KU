Students of the Data Science programme at KU share positive experiences. The student team "Fantastic Four" (Denis, Ali, Veronika, Ruslan) competed in national and international hackathons inspired by their coursework, praising the hands-on courses and support from KU. Jan Stüwe of the Data Science Student Council values the international atmosphere and how the shared English language helps communication and language improvement. Other students highlight that professors are genuinely passionate about teaching, easily accessible via Zoom, email, or personal appointments, and that the programme is well structured with subjects well distributed across semesters. Students also note that the carefully designed course order and the mathematics courses, though demanding, support understanding of the specialist subjects.

type: faq
name: Studying Mathematics Alongside Data Science
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
At KU, Data Science students have the option to pursue a second degree in Mathematics in parallel with their Data Science studies. Mathematics is an integral part of data science but is also interesting in its own right, and the professors are very supportive of students who wish to obtain the second degree, doing what they can to make the process as smooth as possible.

type: structure
name: Master Data Science Study Plan With Semester Abroad
programme: data_science_master
---
The Master Data Science at KU offers an alternative study plan that includes a semester abroad in the third semester. In this variant, the Studium.Pro module is taken in the first semester instead, the third semester abroad is filled with General Electives, and the fourth semester is dedicated entirely to the Master Thesis (30 ECTS). This allows students to gain international experience without extending their studies.

===CHUNK===
type: exam_rule
name: Data Science German Grading Scale
programme: general
---
At KU, as at all German universities, grades range from 1.0 (best) to 4.0 (minimum passing grade), with grades above 4.0 considered a fail. This scale applies to modules, examinations, and final degree grades in the Data Science programmes. For the Master, admission generally requires a Bachelor's grade equivalent to a German 2.0 or better.

===CHUNK===
type: exam_rule
name: Data Science Bachelor Credits and Thesis
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
The Bachelor Data Science at KU requires 180 ECTS in total over 6 semesters. The Bachelor Thesis is worth 10 ECTS and the Bachelor Seminar 5 ECTS, together forming the bachelor seminar and thesis area (15 ECTS). The required area covers 110 ECTS, the specialization 25 ECTS, the general required elective area 15 ECTS, and practical training 10 ECTS.

===CHUNK===
type: programme_info
name: Data Science Bachelor Career Fields
programme: data_science_bachelor
---
Well-trained data scientists are in high demand. Graduates of the Data Science Bachelor at KU can work both methodically (for example as a Data Scientist or Software Engineer) and strategically (for example as a Data Strategist) in fields such as the finance sector, the IT industry, the automotive industry, the biotechnology industry, and in startups and NGOs. The programme also prepares graduates for a Master's degree and a possible subsequent academic career in computer science, statistics, mathematics, or application areas.

Note: this concerns Mathematics teaching degrees (Lehramt), which are separate from the Data Science programme and taught in German. At KU, the teaching degree for Gymnasium (grammar school) in Mathematics is designed for nine semesters, with second-subject options including Economics, Psychology, Catholic Religious Education, Latin, English, German, and Philosophy/Ethics. If Economics or Psychology is chosen as the second subject, a Bachelor in Mathematics can be obtained alongside the state examination with little extra effort. Teaching degrees for Realschule, Grundschule, or Mittelschule are designed for seven semesters with various subject combinations. The Lehramt Mathematics advisor is Dr. Thilo Kuessner.

name: Mathematics Bachelor Detailed Study Plan
programme: mathematics
---
Note: this is the Mathematics Bachelor study plan, a separate programme from Data Science, taught in German, with 180 ECTS over 6 semesters (30 per semester). Semester 1: Analysis I (10 ECTS), Linear Algebra I (10 ECTS), Introduction to Programming Techniques (5 ECTS), Introduction to Statistical Work (5 ECTS). Semester 2: Analysis II (10 ECTS), Linear Algebra II and Analytic Geometry (10 ECTS), Focus subject (Ausrichtung, 10 ECTS). Semester 3: Basics of Algebra (5 ECTS), Integration Theory (5 ECTS), Introduction to Linear Optimization (5 ECTS), Introduction to Stochastics (5 ECTS), Focus subject (10 ECTS). Semester 4: Introduction to Numerical Mathematics (5 ECTS), Integer Optimization (5 ECTS), Probability Theory (5 ECTS), Differential Equations I (5 ECTS), Focus subject (10 ECTS). Semester 5: Applied Statistics with R (10 ECTS), Introduction to Scientific Computing (5 ECTS), Complex Analysis I (5 ECTS), Focus subject (10 ECTS). Semester 6: Bachelor Thesis (10 ECTS), Bachelor Seminar (5 ECTS), Mathematical Programming (5 ECTS), Algebra with Galois Theory (5 ECTS), Complex Analysis II (5 ECTS).

===CHUNK===
type: programme_info
name: Mathematics Master Degree Types at KU
programme: mathematics
---
Note: this concerns the Mathematics Master, separate from Data Science, studied within the Interdisciplinary Master at KU. If Mathematics is taken as the major subject with the extension in Business Mathematics or Applications in Physical Geography, students earn a Master of Science in four semesters. If Mathematics is combined with other subjects such as language or literature studies, students earn a Master of Arts. Application for the Interdisciplinary Master with Mathematics is required by early September, and study begins in the winter semester.
